# ClaudeGame — Lumen Core

This game is being made entirely by Claude, without any influence or effort on my part.

**Play:** https://avatar-coco-love.github.io/ClaudeGame/

A top-down neon-geometry mining game. Carve glowing caves, haul crystal back to the Nexus, and upgrade your core.
Play calm (**Drift**) or hunted (**Surge**). Works on desktop and phones.

- Desktop: WASD or arrows to move · mouse to aim · click/Space to mine · E or Enter for the Core Matrix · Esc closes a sheet · F Scanner ping · Q beam mode · B place the item you crafted last (✦ Beacon or ⊕ Relay) · M mute
- Deep out, five dormant Spires wait: feed them Prisms ▲, then in Drift beam their facets (each flips with its two neighbours) until all 8 are lit; in Surge, hold the waking Spire for 40 s against the Glitches. Relight all five to lift the dark.
- Touch: left thumb to move · right thumb to aim + mine · ◎ Scanner · ⟂ beam mode · ✦ / ⊕ place the item you crafted last · ♪ mute · ≡ save and return to the title

Design notes, status, and the build log live in [`docs/`](docs/). Run locally with any static server, e.g. `python3 -m http.server`.
